Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Naatilevide handles information when you visit and use our Kerala place-discovery website.

Last updated: November 15, 2026

1. Information We May Process

Naatilevide does not require users to create an account or sign in to browse the website.

Some features, such as Nearby Places, may request access to your device's location through your browser. Your location is used to help display nearby destinations. This static website does not intentionally transmit your precise location to a Naatilevide server.

If you contact us through email, we may process the information you voluntarily provide.

2. Browser Storage

Certain website features, such as saved or favourite places, may use local browser storage on your device. This information is stored locally in your browser and is not intentionally uploaded to Naatilevide servers. Clearing your browser data may remove saved information.

3. Cookies, Analytics and Third-Party Services

Naatilevide may use third-party services to operate and improve the website. These services may process technical information such as your IP address, browser or device details, cookie identifiers, and interactions with the website, in accordance with their own policies.

  • Google Analytics: If enabled, Google Analytics helps us understand website traffic and how visitors use the website.
  • Google AdSense: If enabled, Google AdSense may display advertisements on the website. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, personalize where applicable, limit, and measure advertisements, subject to your settings, applicable requirements, and Google's policies.
  • OpenStreetMap: Map tiles or related map services may be requested from OpenStreetMap providers when you use map features.
  • External map links: Links may open services such as Google Maps. Those services are governed by their own privacy policies.
  • Third-party images and fonts: Some visual content or fonts may be loaded from external providers, who may receive technical request information.

For more information about Google's data practices, please review the Google Privacy Policy .

You can review or manage available advertising personalization settings through Google Ads Settings . Your browser may also provide controls to block or delete cookies. Disabling cookies or third-party services may affect some website functions or advertising.

4. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain the website.
  • Improve website performance and user experience.
  • Understand website traffic where analytics is enabled.
  • Display and measure advertisements where advertising is enabled.
  • Respond to enquiries or privacy requests.

Do not send passwords, payment details, identity documents, or other sensitive personal information through general contact channels.

5. Data Retention and Your Choices

Information voluntarily sent through email may remain in the relevant email account until it is no longer required, subject to applicable legal requirements.

  • You can deny or revoke location permission through your browser settings.
  • You can clear saved places by managing your browser's local storage.
  • You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings.
  • You can review Google's available advertising controls through Google Ads Settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Naatilevide is a general travel and place-discovery website. It is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

7. External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites and services. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, content, or policies. Please review their privacy policies before using them.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when website features, services, or applicable requirements change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
