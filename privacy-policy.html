Privacy Policy

This page explains how Naatilevide handles information when you browse the website.

Last updated: November 13, 2026

1. Information we may process

The website may process information you choose to send through email or contact links. Some features, such as nearby-place discovery, may ask your browser for location permission. Your precise location is used in your browser to support the requested feature and is not intentionally sent to Naatilevide servers by this static website.

2. Browser storage

Features such as saved places or travel collections may use local browser storage on your device. Clearing browser data may remove these saved items.

3. Third-party services

Map links, map tiles, images, fonts, analytics (if enabled), and embedded content may be provided by third parties. Their own privacy policies and terms apply when you use those services. The website may link to services such as OpenStreetMap and Google Maps.

4. How information is used

Information you voluntarily provide may be used to respond to your enquiry, maintain the website, or address reports. Do not send passwords, payment details, identity documents, or other sensitive information through general contact channels.

5. Data retention and your choices

Information received by email is retained in the relevant email account until it is no longer needed or you request deletion, subject to applicable legal requirements. You can deny location permission and manage or clear local browser storage through your browser settings.

6. Children

This website is a general travel and place-discovery resource and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

7. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as website features change. For privacy questions or requests, contact naatilevide.in@gmail.com.
