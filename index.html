Travel season basics

October–February: Often more comfortable for many outdoor trips.

March–May: Hotter conditions in many areas; high ranges may feel cooler.

June–September: Southwest monsoon; waterfalls and greenery increase, but heavy rain, landslides and access closures are possible.

These are broad regional patterns, not guarantees for a specific place.
